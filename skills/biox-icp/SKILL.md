# BioX ICP

Use this skill for account selection, lead scoring, sector prioritization, and disqualification.

## Core sectors
Prioritize emissions-intensive and carbon-regulated industries where BioX can solve a material compliance, carbon-accounting, MRV, target-tracking, or environmental-asset problem. Initial priority sectors include cement, steel, aluminium, oil & gas/refining, chemicals, fertilizers, power, and other CCTS/CBAM-exposed industries.

## Priority buyer personas
1. Chief Sustainability Officer / Head of Sustainability / ESG Head
2. Decarbonization / Net Zero / Climate Strategy leadership
3. Environment / EHS leadership when emissions and compliance ownership sits there
4. Operations or manufacturing leadership when plant-level data is central
5. Finance / strategy where carbon cost, capital allocation, or climate risk is material
6. Digital / innovation / IT as technical stakeholders, not default economic buyers
7. CSR/project teams only when the use case is environmental projects, pilots, or asset MRV

## Strong-fit account signals
- Material Scope 1 and/or Scope 2 emissions
- CCTS, CBAM, ETS, carbon-tax, or supply-chain exposure
- Public net-zero/decarbonization target
- Multi-plant or multi-entity emissions data complexity
- Existing ESG reporting but fragmented calculation workflows
- Carbon projects, offsets, environmental assets, or nature-based interventions
- Active sustainability hiring, climate capex, new plants, international exports, or regulatory pressure

## Disqualifiers
Do not prioritize accounts merely because they publish ESG reports. Deprioritize when there is no credible emissions/data/compliance problem, no relevant buyer, no commercial pathway, or the organization is too small to justify enterprise sales effort.

## Scoring
Score each account 0-5 on: regulatory urgency, emissions intensity, data complexity, buyer accessibility, commercial capacity, and timing signal. Prioritize accounts with the strongest combined business pain and path to action.
