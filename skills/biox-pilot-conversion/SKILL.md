# BioX Pilot Conversion

Use this skill when moving a qualified account from interest to a paid pilot or LOI.

## Commercial path
Prospect → qualified conversation → discovery → tailored demo → data/workflow scoping → pilot proposal → paid pilot or LOI → enterprise contract.

## Discovery must establish
- Current carbon-accounting/MRV workflow
- Data sources and ownership
- Calculation pain points and auditability gaps
- Regulatory or target-driven urgency
- Stakeholders and decision process
- Desired outcome and success metric
- Pilot-ready dataset or process
- Timeline, budget path, procurement constraints

## Pilot design
Prefer narrow, measurable pilots over broad free trials. Define:
- One business unit/site/workflow
- Data required
- BioX scope
- Timeline
- Success criteria
- Commercial value
- Owner on both sides
- Conversion condition to broader rollout

## Pilot success examples
- Reduce manual calculation/reconciliation effort
- Produce traceable emissions outputs from source data
- Improve reporting/audit readiness
- Track a defined decarbonization target or intervention
- Digitize an environmental project/MRV workflow

## Rules
Do not offer free pilots by default. Do not treat a demo as pipeline progress unless there is an agreed next step. Always end meetings with owner + action + date.
