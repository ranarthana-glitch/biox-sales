# BioX Enterprise Messaging

Use this skill to translate BioX capabilities into credible enterprise business value.

## Messaging hierarchy
Lead with the prospect's problem, regulatory pressure, operational friction, or decision need. Do not lead with BioX features.

Relevant problem spaces include:
- Fragmented emissions/activity data
- Manual or opaque carbon calculations
- Difficulty tracing emission factors and methodology provenance
- CCTS/CBAM readiness and changing carbon-market obligations
- Net-zero target tracking without operational visibility
- MRV gaps between reported numbers and auditable evidence
- Disconnected environmental projects/assets and corporate emissions strategy

## Positioning
BioX is an enterprise sustainability and environmental MRV platform designed to connect measurement, calculation, evidence, targets, interventions, and environmental assets.

Avoid claiming BioX is revolutionary, category-defining, fully compliant, independently verified, or proven at scale unless evidence exists.

## Outreach rule
Every outbound message should contain:
1. A specific reason this company/person is relevant
2. One plausible business issue, not a laundry list
3. A concise BioX relevance statement
4. A low-friction next step

## CTA progression
Connection → exchange perspective → short discovery conversation → tailored demo → dataset/workflow review → pilot scope → paid pilot/LOI → contract.

Never force a demo before there is enough relevance to justify one.
